# Daily Brief & Pre-Meeting Prep

Two views that show you what's coming, what was discussed before, and what to read before a call.

## Daily Brief

The **Daily Brief** sidebar entry shows every meeting in your day on one page. For each:

- Time, title, attendees
- The pre-meeting brief (a short prose summary of the meeting's context — last conversation, open action items, agenda hints)
- Quick links: open the meeting, jump to the conference URL

Use it as a morning glance to know where you're spending the day and which meetings need a few minutes of prep.

The badge on the sidebar entry shows how many of today's meetings still need prep generation.

---

## Pre-Meeting Brief

The **pre-meeting brief** is a short, generated paragraph for one meeting that summarises:

- Who the attendees are and what role they likely play
- The most relevant past meeting(s) with these attendees
- Open action items from those meetings
- Any agenda-style content from the calendar event description

It's generated by an AI call on top of:

- Calendar attendees + RSVP status
- The meeting title + description
- The Person directory (so cross-meeting context survives email/name format changes)
- The most-relevant past meetings (cosine similarity over summaries + transcripts)
- Knowledge base hits (if you have one)

The brief lives at the top of the meeting detail and live-recording views. It's also embedded in the Daily Brief.

### When does it generate?

- **Pre-compute pass:** runs at app launch and roughly every 15 minutes for meetings starting within the next 48 hours, so you almost always have a pre-built brief ready.
- **On-demand:** if you open a meeting and the brief hasn't been generated, it's queued immediately. You'll see "Generating brief..." for ~10–30 seconds.
- **On record start:** generates fresh right before recording begins, in case the calendar event was just edited.

### When the brief is empty

You'll see a placeholder like *"Not enough information to generate a brief"* when:

- The meeting has no description, no related past meetings, no shared attendees
- The AI provider (Claude or Ollama) isn't configured or unreachable

That's okay — recording still works fine. Add attendees or notes and the next refresh will populate.

## Related Past Meetings

Below the pre-meeting brief, the **Related Meetings** section lists past meetings ranked by relevance:

- Same recurring series (highest weight)
- Overlapping attendee sets
- Topical similarity (cosine over summary embeddings)

Click any related meeting to open it. The brief uses these meetings as context, but you can read them directly to refresh your memory.

---

## Configuration

**Settings → AI (Claude)** or **Settings → AI (Local)** — the brief uses the same provider as your summary generator.

**Settings → Prompts → Pre-meeting brief** — customise the prompt template used for brief generation. Defaults are tuned for crispness; tweak if you want a particular style.

**Settings → General → Morning Brief** — opt-in to a daily macOS notification at the time of your choice with the day's schedule.

---

## Privacy

The pre-meeting brief sends to your AI provider:

- Meeting title, description, attendees, time
- Snippets from the most-relevant past meetings (typically 3–5 short summaries)
- Knowledge base chunks if relevant

Nothing is persisted server-side beyond the AI provider's standard request handling. With Ollama, all of this stays on your Mac.
